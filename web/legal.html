Privacy policy

What we keep

  • Account: where you logged in (Google or GitHub) and your user number there, the name you chose on Boardmadang (including past names), when you signed up and changed your name, and suspensions. We don't store your email or profile picture. Your name from the login provider is only used to prefill the name field.
  • Guests: a guest number made for each browser
  • Visits: the days you opened the site, with a hash of your account or guest number made with a key only the server knows. Used only to count daily visitors.
  • Match records: both players' account or guest numbers and names, the moves, the result, and the time. Anyone with a match's address can replay it.
  • Comments and reports: what you wrote, your account, and the time. For reports, the reporting account and the reason
  • Uploaded games: the code, the listing, the uploading account, and the clear replay
  • Moderation log: what moderators did and their notes
  • We don't collect IP addresses, though they may briefly appear in the server's error logs.

Cookies and browser storage

One cookie remembers your login (bm_session, up to 180 days), and others are used only while you log in. Your browser stores your language and emote sound settings. We don't use ads or third-party analytics tools.

How we use it

Only for logging in, matches and replays, handling comments and reports, game uploads, and operating statistics such as visitor counts. We don't use it for ads, and we don't sell or share it. If the law requires us to hand something over, we hand over only that.

Who hosts it

The server and data are on Google Cloud (Seoul region). Google and GitHub handle logins under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

  • We keep it until you delete your account, in the "Account" section of your profile.
  • Deleting your account removes your account, comments, and match list right away. Your opponents' match records keep a nameless "deleted user" seat.
  • Your uploaded games are taken down, but their code is kept so past matches can be replayed. Reports and the moderation log are kept to prevent abuse.
  • Server disk backups keep deleted data for up to 14 more days.
  • Guest match records are kept while the service runs.
  • Visit hashes are deleted after 30 days whether or not you delete your account; only the daily visitor counts remain.

Your rights

You can change your name or delete your account in your profile. To see, correct, or delete anything else, email us. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 14, we delete it.
